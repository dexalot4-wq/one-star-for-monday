## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, no secrets) writes a single
line of text — a one-star review of Monday, written as though Monday
were a restaurant — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> One star. Monday seated me by the kitchen, forgot my coffee, and charged me for the whole week.

The line is 18 words and 95 characters: one natural complaint with
three concrete diner elements — a bad table (seated by the kitchen), a
service failure (forgot my coffee), and a bad bill (charged for the
whole week) — and the bill lands the Monday joke, because Monday is the
day the whole week gets charged.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
